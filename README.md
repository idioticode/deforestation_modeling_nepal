# Deforestation Modeling using an Ensemble Machine Learning Approach: An Insight from Nepal

This repository contains all R scripts and resources related to the deforestation risk modeling study in Nepal. The project applies ensemble machine learning techniques to predict deforestation risk and identify spatial hotspots.

## Study Overview

This study is among the pioneering works on deforestation risk mapping in Nepal. It integrates multiple machine learning models to generate a reliable deforestation risk map that can inform forest governance, conservation planning, and policy interventions.

Citation:
>Pokhrel, S. (2025). *Deforestation Modeling using an Ensemble Machine Learning Approach: An Insight from Nepal*. Authorea (Preprint) DOI: [10.22541/au.175588034.46859557/v1](https://doi.org/10.22541/au.175588034.46859557/v1)

## Key Findings

- Approximately one million hectares of Nepal’s forests are under deforestation risk.
- Biophysical factors primarily drive deforestation at higher elevations, while anthropogenic factors dominate in lower elevations.
- Fragmented forests are consistently at greater risk regardless of location.
- Contrary to existing assumptions, Siwalik and Terai regions are not the only highly vulnerable zones.
- The risk proportion in each region remains below 5%, suggesting that the majority of forests are not at very high risk.

## Applications

- Policy-making: Guiding land-use planning and conservation strategies.
- Forest management: Supporting REDD+ initiatives and sustainable practices.
- Replication: The models can be adapted for other regions of the Hindu Kush Himalaya with similar biophysical and socioeconomic settings.

## Limitations and Future Directions

- Exclusion of some socioeconomic and anthropogenic variables due to data unavailability (e.g., timber price, rural roads).
- Forest recovery was not incorporated into the model.
- The predicted risk map does not specify time horizons of deforestation.
- Future studies should integrate field data, higher-resolution predictors, and interaction effects among variables.
